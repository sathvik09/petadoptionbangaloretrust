Why we rescue

From suffering to a chance at life.

Many animals reach us after accidents, neglect, abuse, illness or abandonment. Their road to recovery can require urgent veterinary treatment, medicines, surgery, boarding and months of care.

Behind every rescue is a living being who feels pain, fear and love. Our goal is to turn that fear into safety and that suffering into hope.

“A rescue is not just saving an animal from the street. It is giving them the opportunity to know what kindness feels like.”
